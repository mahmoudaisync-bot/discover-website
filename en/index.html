FAQ

Answers to our clients' most common questions

Which types of companies do you help set up?

We help set up local companies of all types, as well as branches of foreign and GCC companies. Tell us about your activity and we will explain the available options.

How long does a transaction take?

Timelines vary by transaction type and the relevant authority's procedures, so we do not commit to fixed durations. We do explain the expected steps and keep you updated as your request progresses.

How is the service cost determined?

Costs depend on the service and the related government fees. We provide a proposal after reviewing your request.

Can I start while I am outside the UAE?

Yes. We accept requests from clients abroad and communicate with them remotely. Whether personal attendance is needed at any stage is determined by the relevant authority, depending on the transaction.

Do you serve contracting and engineering consultancy firms?

Yes. We handle classification files for contracting and engineering consultancy firms across all grades, as well as their formation and licensing.

What documents do I need to get started?

Documents vary by service. Send us your request and we will share the list of documents required for your case.
