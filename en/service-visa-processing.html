Government & Banking Transactions

What is the service?

This service covers residence visa procedures linked to the company, such as partner, investor and employee visas, and family residence. We prepare and follow the applications with the authorities and coordinate medical test and biometrics appointments, until the residence and Emirates ID are issued.
